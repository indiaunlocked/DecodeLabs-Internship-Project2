Plant Care 101

Simple rules that keep your plants alive and thriving.

01

Water Wisely

More plants die from overwatering than drought. Check if the top inch of soil is dry before watering. Most indoor plants need water just once or twice a week.

02

Light is Food

Match your plant to available light. South-facing windows = bright light. North-facing = low light. Rotate pots quarterly for even sun exposure.

03

Right Soil, Right Pot

Always use pots with drainage holes. Succulents need sandy, fast-draining mix. Tropical plants love rich, moisture-retaining soil. Never use garden soil indoors.

04

Humidity Matters

Most tropical houseplants need humid environments. Mist leaves regularly or use a water tray — especially during dry Indian summers.
